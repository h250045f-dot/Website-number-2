About Us

Who we are, what we believe in and the people who keep the club running.

Who we are

The HIT Gaming Club was founded in 2024 by a group of students who wanted a structured, welcoming place to play, compete and learn about the games industry.

Our vision

To be the leading student gaming community in Zimbabwe, turning passion for games into skills, friendships and careers.

Our mission

To provide an inclusive platform where students can play, compete, create and grow through gaming and technology.

Our objectives

  • Host regular tournaments and game nights
  • Teach game development and design skills
  • Promote teamwork, fair play and healthy gaming
  • Represent HIT in inter-university esports

Club rules

  1. Respect every member - no harassment or toxic behaviour.
  2. Play fair - cheating leads to disqualification.
  3. Look after lab equipment and venues.
  4. Balance gaming with your studies.
